How post-construction cleanup works

Construction cleanup isn't one pass with a broom. We phase it around your schedule so the space is ready when the owner walks it.

Rough clean

While trades are still working: debris removal, bulk trash haul-off, sweeping and first dust control, so the crews on site can work safely.

Final clean

After the last trade is done: floors, walls, baseboards, cabinets, countertops, fixtures and windows brought up to move-in standard.

Punch-list clean

Right before the walkthrough: paint overspray, adhesive residue, fingerprints and dust that settles again after the final trades wrap.

Add to any phase: floor stripping and waxing, windows on the second story and up, pressure washing, and HEPA vacuums for fine dust.
