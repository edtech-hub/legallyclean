About Legally Clean

A woman-owned commercial cleaning company, founded by Sherry W. Rudolph in 2006 and based in Lauderhill.

Our story

Twenty years of job sites

Sherry Rudolph started Legally Clean in 2006. In 2026 the company turns 20, with offices in Lauderhill, Miami and West Palm Beach and crews working with construction firms, government entities and healthcare facilities across the tri-county area.

People in the industry call her the Pink Construction Hat Diva. She built the business on relationships and deadlines. Every construction job ends with a walkthrough date, and her systems are set up around hitting it.

That track record earned her recognition as a US Department of Commerce Firm of the Year recipient.

How we hire

We hire with a mission: real jobs for people who face real barriers to employment.

It comes from Sherry's 20-plus years as an employment specialist and her Bachelor of Social Work from Saginaw Valley State University.
